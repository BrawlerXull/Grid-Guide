Restoration Alerts, Contact Options, and Your Privacy

Brightvale Electric Customer Help Center. How to get outage updates by text, the ways to reach Brightvale Electric, and how we protect your information.

How do I sign up for restoration text alerts?

Restoration alerts send text messages about an outage at your address, including updates to the estimated restoration time and a message when power is restored. GridGuide can sign you up in seconds with your mobile number and ZIP code. Standard message rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to cancel, or HELP for help.

How can I contact Brightvale Electric?

  • Emergencies (downed lines, sparks, fire): call 911 and our Emergency Line at (800) 555-0111, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
  • GridGuide chat assistant: 24 hours a day on our website.
  • Customer Service: (800) 555-0100, 7 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday, and 24 hours a day during declared storm emergencies.
  • Request a callback: GridGuide can book a callback from a Customer Care specialist at a time that suits you.
  • Email: help@brightvale-electric.example. We reply within 1 business day.
  • Medical Priority line: (800) 555-0155.

Can I talk to a person?

Yes. At any time you can ask GridGuide to connect you with a person. Depending on the time of day and the reason for your request, GridGuide can transfer you to a live specialist, book a callback, or send your request to our Customer Care team by email. Your conversation history is shared with the specialist so you do not have to repeat yourself.

How do you protect my privacy?

  • To help with outages, alerts, and payment extensions, we only ask for your ZIP code and the last 4 digits of the phone number on your account.
  • We will never ask for your full account number, Social Security number, passwords, PINs, or bank or card details in chat. If you are asked for these, it is not Brightvale Electric.
  • Brightvale Electric will never threaten to disconnect your power within the hour unless you pay by gift card, cryptocurrency, or wire transfer. These are scams. Hang up and contact us using the numbers on this page.
