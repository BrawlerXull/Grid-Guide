Medical Priority Program

Brightvale Electric Customer Help Center. Support for customers who rely on powered medical equipment at home.

What is the Medical Priority program?

The Medical Priority program supports customers who rely on electrically powered medical equipment at home, such as oxygen concentrators, home dialysis machines, ventilators, CPAP machines, electric wheelchairs, or refrigerated medicine.

What benefits do Medical Priority customers receive?

  • Priority callbacks during outages from our Medical Priority team, usually within 15 minutes during declared storm emergencies.
  • Advance notice at least 48 hours before planned maintenance outages.
  • Check-in calls during extended outages.
  • Extra time before any disconnection for non-payment, and help finding bill assistance.

Does Medical Priority guarantee my power will be restored first?

No. Restoration follows a safety-based order that restores the most customers as quickly as possible. Medical Priority customers must have a backup plan, such as a battery backup, a generator used safely outdoors, or a place to go such as a family member's home or a hospital. If you are in danger or your medical equipment has stopped and your life is at risk, call 911 immediately.

Who qualifies and how do I enroll?

Any residential customer with a household member who relies on powered medical equipment or refrigerated medicine can apply. To enroll, complete the Medical Priority application on our website and have it signed by a licensed medical provider. GridGuide can also start your request and arrange a callback from the Medical Priority team. Enrollment is confirmed within 5 business days and must be renewed every 2 years.

Medical Priority line: (800) 555-0155, available 24 hours a day during storm emergencies and 8 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday, at other times.

I rely on medical equipment and my power is out right now. What should I do?

If anyone is in danger, call 911 first. Then contact our Medical Priority line at (800) 555-0155 or tell GridGuide that someone in your home relies on medical equipment, and we will arrange a priority callback. Move to your backup plan if the outage may last longer than your equipment's battery.
