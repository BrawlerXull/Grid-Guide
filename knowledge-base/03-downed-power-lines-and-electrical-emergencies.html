Downed Power Lines and Electrical Emergencies

Brightvale Electric Customer Help Center. What to do if you see a downed power line, sparks, fire, or another electrical emergency. These situations are emergencies and must not be handled through chat.

What should I do if I see a downed power line?

  • Assume every downed line is live and dangerous, even if it is not sparking or making noise.
  • Stay at least 35 feet away, about the length of a school bus. Keep children and pets away.
  • Call 911 and the Brightvale Electric 24-hour Emergency Line at (800) 555-0111 immediately.
  • Never touch a downed line or anything touching it, such as a fence, tree, puddle, or vehicle.
  • Never try to move a line with a stick, broom, or any other object.

Downed lines must be reported by phone, not through chat or email, so that we can send a crew immediately.

What if a power line falls on my car?

Stay inside the vehicle and call 911. Warn others to stay away. Only leave the vehicle if it is on fire. If you must leave, jump clear with both feet together without touching the car and the ground at the same time, then shuffle away with small steps, keeping your feet together.

Other electrical emergencies

Call 911 and our Emergency Line at (800) 555-0111 right away if you see any of the following:

  • Sparks, fire, or smoke from power lines, poles, transformers, or your electric meter.
  • A tree or branch touching or leaning on a power line.
  • A damaged pole or an open, damaged, or humming electrical equipment box.
  • Flooding near electrical equipment or in a home with electricity still on.

What if I smell gas?

Brightvale Electric provides electricity only. If you smell gas, leave the area immediately, do not use light switches or phones inside the building, and call 911 and your gas provider from a safe place.

Flooded homes

Never enter a flooded basement or room if the electricity may still be on. Do not use electrical appliances that have been in water until a licensed electrician has inspected them. Call our Emergency Line if you need power to your home disconnected for safety.
