Storm Damage, Home Repairs, and Claims

Brightvale Electric Customer Help Center. Who repairs what after a storm, and how to submit a claim for damage or spoiled food.

Who is responsible for repairs to the equipment on my home?

  • Brightvale Electric repairs and maintains the power lines, poles, transformers, the service drop (the overhead wire running from the pole to your home), and the electric meter.
  • The customer is responsible for the weatherhead (the pipe and cap where the service drop connects to your house), the meter base or meter box, and all wiring inside the home. These must be repaired by a licensed electrician before we can safely restore power.

If the weatherhead or meter base on your home was damaged, our crew will tell you and leave a notice. Call us once your electrician has finished the repair and we will reconnect your service.

Can I get reimbursed for spoiled food or damaged appliances?

  • Outages caused by storms, severe weather, or other events outside our control are generally not eligible for reimbursement.
  • If an outage lasting more than 24 hours was caused by a Brightvale Electric equipment failure or error, you may submit a claim for spoiled food of up to $250 per household.
  • Claims for damaged appliances or property are reviewed case by case.

Storm Relief Credit

During a declared storm emergency, residential customers whose power was out for more than 72 hours in a row receive an automatic one-time Storm Relief Credit of $50 on their next bill. You do not need to apply.

How do I submit a claim?

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the outage using the claim form on our website or by email to help@brightvale-electric.example. Include your outage ticket number if you have one, a description of the damage, and receipts or photos. Our Claims team reviews every claim and responds within 10 business days. GridGuide can explain the process and connect you with the Claims team, but it cannot approve or deny claims.
