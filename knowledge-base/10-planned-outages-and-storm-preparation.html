Planned Outages and Storm Preparation

Brightvale Electric Customer Help Center. How planned maintenance outages work and how to prepare your household before a storm.

What is a planned outage?

Sometimes we need to turn off power to upgrade or repair equipment safely. We notify affected customers at least 48 hours in advance by mail, phone, or text. Medical Priority customers receive an additional phone call. Planned outages usually last 2 to 4 hours and are scheduled during the day when possible.

How should I prepare for a storm?

  • Sign up for restoration text alerts before the storm arrives.
  • Charge phones, laptops, and portable batteries.
  • Keep flashlights, fresh batteries, a battery-powered radio, and a first-aid kit ready.
  • Store at least 3 days of drinking water (about 1 gallon per person per day) and non-perishable food.
  • Fill coolers with ice and set your refrigerator and freezer to their coldest settings.
  • If someone in your home relies on medical equipment, confirm your backup plan and enroll in the Medical Priority program.
  • Know how to open your garage door manually.
  • Save our Emergency Line, (800) 555-0111, in your phone.

Trees and power lines

Trees touching power lines are a leading cause of outages. Brightvale Electric trims trees near power lines on a regular schedule. Never trim trees near power lines yourself. To request a free inspection of a tree near a power line, contact Customer Service.

Streetlights

To report a streetlight that is out, flickering, or damaged, contact Customer Service with the nearest street address or intersection. Streetlight repairs are usually completed within 5 business days, except during storm emergencies.
