How Power Restoration Works

Brightvale Electric Customer Help Center. How Brightvale Electric decides which repairs happen first after a storm, and why some areas get power back before others.

In what order do you restore power?

Brightvale Electric restores power in an order designed to get the most people back on safely and as quickly as possible:

  • 1. Public safety hazards first: downed lines, wires on roads, and situations where life or property is in immediate danger.
  • 2. Critical facilities: hospitals, emergency services, water treatment plants, and emergency shelters.
  • 3. Transmission lines and substations, which can restore power to thousands of customers at once.
  • 4. Main distribution lines (feeders) that serve the largest numbers of customers.
  • 5. Smaller tap lines and neighborhood transformers.
  • 6. Individual service lines to single homes and businesses.

Does the Medical Priority program mean my power is restored first?

No. Medical Priority customers receive priority callbacks and check-in calls, but power restoration follows the order above because one repair can restore power to many homes at once. If someone in your home relies on powered medical equipment, you should always have a backup plan. If anyone is in danger, call 911.

Why can restoration take a long time after a big storm?

  • Crews must first make the area safe and assess all damage before repairs begin.
  • Fallen trees often have to be cleared before lines can be repaired.
  • Crews cannot work in high winds, lightning, or flooding.
  • Broken poles and damaged transformers can take several hours each to replace.

During very large storms, Brightvale Electric requests extra crews from neighboring utilities through mutual aid agreements.

Typical restoration times

These are typical ranges, not promises. Your actual restoration time depends on the damage in your area.

  • Small, local outages (a single transformer or line): usually 2 to 6 hours.
  • Moderate storms (up to 10% of customers affected): most customers restored within 24 hours.
  • Major storms (more than 10% of customers affected): most customers restored within 2 to 3 days, with some remote areas taking longer.

Can I ask a crew for an exact time?

Crews focus on making repairs safely. Please do not approach crews or work areas. For updates, use GridGuide, our website, or restoration text alerts. We will always give you our best estimate, and we will update it when it changes.
