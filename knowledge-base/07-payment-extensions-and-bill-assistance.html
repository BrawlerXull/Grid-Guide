Payment Extensions and Bill Assistance

Brightvale Electric Customer Help Center. Options for customers who need more time to pay their electric bill, including payment extensions, payment plans, and assistance programs.

Can I get more time to pay my bill?

Yes. Brightvale Electric offers payment extensions for customers who need a little more time to pay.

  • Extensions of up to 14 days are approved automatically if you have not had a payment extension in the last 12 months. GridGuide can set this up for you right away and give you your new due date.
  • Requests for 15 to 30 days, or for a second extension within 12 months, are reviewed by our Customer Care team. GridGuide will create a review request and our team will contact you within 2 business days. Approval is not guaranteed.
  • Extensions longer than 30 days are not available. For larger balances, ask about a payment plan.

There is no fee for a payment extension, and no late fee is charged if you pay by the new due date.

Late fees during storm emergencies

During a declared storm emergency, Brightvale Electric waives late fees for bills due during the emergency period and for 14 days afterwards. Service is not disconnected for non-payment during a declared storm emergency.

Payment plans

If you have a past-due balance, you can spread it over 3 to 12 monthly payments added to your regular bill. Payment plans are set up by our Customer Care team. Ask GridGuide to connect you with Customer Care.

Bill assistance programs

  • Brightvale Helping Hands: a one-time bill credit of up to $150 per year for income-qualified households, funded by customer and employee donations.
  • Budget Billing: pay the same amount every month based on your average yearly usage, so seasonal bills are easier to plan for.
  • Senior and disability discounts: a 10% discount on the energy charge for qualifying customers.

Applications for assistance programs are reviewed by our Customer Care team. We never ask for your full Social Security number or bank details in chat.
